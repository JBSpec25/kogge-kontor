## Description (4000)

Lübeck in the spring of 1356. You found a trading house with an old ship and a few hundred guilders. Fourteen years later, at the Peace of Stralsund, your name should stand at the top.

TRADE LIKE THE HANSA
Twelve ports from London to Novgorod, each with its own goods and its own needs. Salt and herring from Lübeck, stockfish from Bergen, furs from Novgorod, cloth from Bruges. Prices react to every purchase, to the seasons and to events: a hard winter, a feud in Livonia, an embargo of the Hanseatic Diet.

SHIPS, CAPTAINS AND PIRATES
From the small snekke to the great hulk. Hire captains, send convoys on their own routes and arm them with crossbows, trebuchets and the first stone guns. Whoever goes to sea meets pirates and must decide: flee, keep your distance or board.

YOUR TOWN GROWS WITH YOU
Build a Kontor, a brewery and a saltworks, endow a chapel, a crane or a hospital. Your home town grows visibly, house by house.

RISE AND RACE
From pedlar to councillor, mayor and finally Alderman of the Hansa. Three merchant families want the office too. Form alliances, start feuds, win the votes at the Diet and enter your name on your leaderboard.

HISTORY TO PLAY
The years 1356 to 1372 with their real events: plague, war against Valdemar of Denmark, the Confederation of Cologne and the Peace of Stralsund.

## Review notes (English, for App Review)

Kogge & Kontor is a trading game set in the Hanseatic age and runs completely offline. No account needed. The game follows the device language (German, English or Spanish) and can be switched under "Saved games and settings" → "Language".
The first 50 game days are free (until 21 May 1356 in the game). After that, time stands still until the full version is bought (non-consumable in-app purchase "kogge_kontor_vollversion").
To test the purchase: tap "Saved games and settings" at the bottom, section "Full version" → "Unlock full version". "Restore purchase" is right next to it.
